# Rental dates and deletion

In Demo account → My listings, use **Delete item** and confirm to remove your listing. Cancelling keeps it. Past request history remains.

When adding or editing a rental or free-sharing listing, enter **Available from** and **Return by**. Sale listings do not require dates.

Check **Already rented / on loan for some dates**, then enter **Unavailable from** and **Available again on**. You can also select **Rented out / On loan** under My listings to open these fields. Save changes to apply them. Selecting Available clears the owner-entered blocked period; accepted requests continue to reserve their own dates.

Dates are pickup-inclusive and return-exclusive: an item returned on January 15 can be picked up again on January 15. Detail pages and My listings show the availability and blocked periods. Requests outside the availability window or overlapping an owner-entered block or accepted request are rejected. Acceptance rechecks dates. Pending requests do not reserve dates.

Legacy listings remain browsable; editing a rental without an availability window asks the owner to supply one. All listings, photos and booking state remain local to this browser. Clearing demo request history also clears reservations created by those requests. No backend, real authentication or cross-device reservation system is included.

Validation: 11 domain/date checks and browser flows for creation, editing, photos, status, deletion, dates, responsive layouts and reduced motion.
